Brass App

Simple tools for brass and rhythm lessons

Tools

Balloon Breath

Fill the balloon with a big relaxed breath, then empty it slow and steady. Then blow warm air through the mouthpiece.

BreathingMouthpiece air5 levels

Buzz-Along

Hear a long note, breathe, then buzz it back on the mouthpiece. Grows from a first buzz to high and low notes and mini sirens.

BuzzingTrumpet · Trombone · Horn6 levels

Note Names

Meet the first notes and name them. Each instrument reads its own written notes and clef, and hears the real sound.

ReadingDo Re Mi · C D E7 levels

Clap-Along

Hear a rhythm and clap it back, or pick which of three rhythms you heard.

EchoEar quiz9 levels
