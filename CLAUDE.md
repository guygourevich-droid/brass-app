# Brass App

Music teacher's toolkit for brass and rhythm lessons: kids 9–12, projected in class, and installed on an iPad for offline use. Hosted on GitHub Pages from `main` (repo root). Brass-specific tools (trumpet first) are planned but not yet designed.

## Constraints
- Plain HTML/CSS/JS only. No build step, frameworks, CDNs or backend — every page must work offline.
- One tool = one self-contained HTML file at repo root + a tile in `index.html`.
- New or renamed file → add it to `FILES` in `sw.js` and bump `CACHE`, or installed iPads keep serving the old copy.
- Fonts are self-hosted in `fonts/`; never link Google Fonts.
- British note names (crotchet, quaver…). Note names on screen default to fixed do-re-mi (Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Si), with a C D E option.
- Big, clear type — read from a projector. Clap-Along is clean white; the breathing, buzzing and note tools share a playful sky look.
- Transposition matters: students always see their WRITTEN note (trumpet in B♭ and horn in F in treble clef, trombone in bass clef); sounds play at CONCERT pitch (trumpet −2, horn −7 semitones, trombone 0).
- Discuss and agree what a new tool does with the teacher before building it.
- Classes are 4-6 absolute beginners, ages 9-12, one laptop + projector. Lesson 1 asks "did a sound happen?", not "was it the right note".
- Traffic light, everywhere it appears: red = stop/instrument down, yellow = get ready and breathe, green = play. Manual, teacher-controlled. Never flash it, never faster than one change per 250 ms, and never let colour be the only channel (position, shape, fill-vs-outline, size, word).
- The microphone may only ever add a glow. Silence is a waiting state, never failure; nothing turns red; no individual score reaches the projector; the counter counts attempts and only rises.
- New pages are Hebrew (`lang="he" dir="rtl"`), near-wordless, and use Rubik. Keep music notation `direction: ltr` or the clef mirrors. Keyboard shortcuts read `e.code`, never `e.key` (a Hebrew layout turns F into כ).
- Trumpet, trombone and horn classes have different teachers: on-screen tips stay universal (posture, breath, steady air, rest), no instrument-specific technique.

- iPad microphone (all seven mic pages, same block after `"use strict"`): the analyser is pulled to `destination` through a zero gain (old WebKit fed unpulled analysers zeros), the engine is recreated if the mic's sample rate differs, any tap resumes a suspended/interrupted engine, `getFloatTimeDomainData` is shimmed for Safari < 14.1, and exact-zero input for 3 s shows "אין קול מהמיקרופון". Tapping the mic status opens the check panel (no keyboard on iPad); its first lines: engine state, rate and whether its clock moves (זז/עומד); the mic track state, rate, raw vs voice and ec/agc; the raw level in dB. iPad/iPhone (`IS_IOS`; iPadOS Safari claims to be a Mac, so touch points decide) open the mic in voice mode (`audio: true`: voice processing + automatic gain) — raw mode was measured far too quiet on the teacher's iPad (−58 dB with a horn playing; voice mode worked). Desktop keeps raw. `?voice=1` / `?voice=0` force either. The iOS Simulator gets no Mac microphone input here — it can test WebKit code paths, not real sound.

## Map
- `index.html` — home screen, tool tiles, service worker registration.
- Pause: every game has ⏸ in the top bar and Esc (Esc/Space/tap resumes). Long-note, breathing and buzzing skip their frame loop while `paused`. Ninja, Songs, Right Note, Horn Ladder and Clap-Along use a "Pause" block after `"use strict"` that freezes the page clock (wraps `performance.now` and `requestAnimationFrame`) and suspends the audio engine (`pauseAudio()`), so timers, counts and scheduled sounds all wait; their pause screen keeps a "■ stop" link where Esc used to stop a round. Not in Note Names (no clock) or the traffic light (Esc = lights off).
- `balloon-breath.html` — "נשימה", breathing: one press (Space / clicker / tap the picture) = one breath, nothing starts by itself. Breathe in 4 → blow out 4/8/12/16 (`LEVELS`), no hold. Two `WORLDS`, same breath: 🫧 bubble (in: wand dips into the soap; out: one bubble grows from the ring at a steady rate, bigger for longer levels; end: it floats up to a `SLOTS` place in the sky) and 🌼 dandelion (in: bud opens into a seed ball; out: the breeze takes the seeds one by one, the last as the breath ends; end: a yellow flower grows in the meadow). N clears the sky/meadow. Microphone optional, asked on the first breath: blowing heard → golden breeze, a little faster, never anything else. The teacher rejected a windmill → electricity → village version: kids don't connect windmills to power; the breath must touch the thing directly. `?test=1` + `testBlow(gain)`, `?world=dandelion`.
- `buzz-along.html` — "זמזום", mouthpiece buzzing with a bee, in the teacher's own order. 1 🐝 any buzz flies the bee across the meadow, buds open as it passes (no pitch). 2 🌸 the app buzzes a note (listen first), a flower grows to that height; the bee's height = the child's pitch; in the band (±80 cents, out at 110) the bee flies over and lands → honey drop to the jar; after a success a big button (פרח הבא / עוד מסלול) or Space moves on (`TARGETS`, semitones above the first buzz). Bee starts at the left edge, flower at the right end. 3 🍯 same, stay landed 4 or 8 s to fill the honeycomb; off the pitch only pauses it. 4 〰️ free: the bee draws its pitch as a trail; route (a 2nd, 3rd or 5th): the starting buzz plays with a 3-2-1, then the bee flies on at a steady speed home to the hive over a tree, a house and a sunflower, one siren each (`routeSemi`); the buzz lifts it, silent it glides back to the starting height (the teacher wanted it to keep going, not pause); dots only over the obstacles, the flat stretches are for breathing; too low over an obstacle → the bee bumps, gets dizzy (stars, soft boing) and the route restarts — the teacher asked for this, an agreed exception to "silence/nothing fails"; free-draw ✏️ is the last option, 〰 2 the default; dots bloom when met, a top map shows the way, the hive ends it. `INSTRUMENTS` = concert first-buzz pitch, chosen by the teacher: trumpet B♭3 (written low C), horn F3 (written low C below the staff), trombone F3; `TARGETS` go a little down as well as up. Model buzz = synthetic (bright pulse wave, formant lifts, breath, scoop, wobble) unless `sounds/buzz/<instrument>.m4a` exists (a steady buzz on the first note; pitch-shifted). Same listening as the long-note game (tone + 12 dB + steady onset; floor frozen while buzzing). `?test=1` + `testBuzz(semitones, gain)` / `testNoise(gain)`.
- `note-names.html` — note reading: `INSTRUMENTS` holds clef, transposition and first notes in teaching order (trumpet/horn written C4→C5, trombone B♭2→B♭3); level n = first n+1 notes. Clefs/notes drawn with the Bravura font (SMuFL code points).
- `ramzor.html` — the traffic light: three lamps (octagon/triangle/circle), teacher-controlled, optional hold bar, works from a presentation clicker. No microphone, no network: this is the fallback if anything else fails.
- `sound-game.html` — "צליל ארוך", the long-note game: one steady note flies a rocket up from the ground into space; the world scrolls past (birds, plane, clouds, satellite, astronaut, Moon, Saturn, comet, UFO…) and the sky darkens with height. `LEVELS` = 4/8/12/16 s (ending at the sun above the clouds, satellite, Moon, Saturn) and ∞ (as far as you can: big seconds count up, and back home it shows the turn's longest note — the teacher asked for this number, an agreed exception to "no individual score"; places reached this turn also stay in a top-corner row). `CHECKS` = places every 4 s (new ones through 60 s via `DEEP`, then again in new `HUES`), `SIGHTS` = things on the way; the sky goes day → night → purple → teal → magenta (`#sky2`…`#sky7`), with a bigger cheer every 20 s. No pitch and no instrument choice: a note starts when a clear tone (80–1000 Hz) is 12 dB over the noise floor and steady for 250 ms (talk, claps and breath do not count), keeps going down to 6 dB with 300 ms gaps forgiven, and the floor never rises while a note sounds. Stopping → parachute home; arriving celebrates once and waits for silence. `?test=1` + `testSound(gain)` / `testNoise(gain)` in the console.
- `right-note.html` — the staff exercise: target note + the note actually played, teacher picks which written notes are in play (per instrument, saved in localStorage), hold to win, then it moves to another chosen note. Hebrew note names only.
- `horn-ladder.html` — "סולם", open notes (no valves; trombone 1st position) as ladder rungs, for all three instruments (`INSTRUMENTS`, shared `brass-inst`). Horn: open F horn partials, written C3…G5 = partials 2–12, 11 skipped, a long ladder. Trumpet (written C4 G4 C5 E5 G5) and trombone (B♭2 F3 B♭3 D4 F4): partials 2–6, a short ladder of far-apart rungs, 2–5 shown by default; rungs with no recording borrow the nearest one, pitch-shifted (`sampleHz` reads the pitch from the sample's name). Settings saved per instrument (horn keeps `horn-ladder`). Rungs placed half by pitch, half evenly. A monkey climbs to the partial being played after it is steady 300 ms; banana mode = target rung, hold to win. Targets are the natural partial pitches (B♭4 ≈ 31 cents flat), not equal temperament. Reference = real horn recordings in `sounds/horn/` (Univ. of Iowa MIS ff, no restrictions; named by CONCERT pitch, `off` = measured cents per recording, corrected at playback). `?test=horn` loops a recording into the detector; `testTone(n, cents)` in the console. Noise floor does not rise while a note is heard (a long tone otherwise goes deaf after ~15 s — the other mic pages still have that behaviour).
- `ninja.html` — Note Ninja, all three instruments: one child per round of 3–10 notes from right-note's shared picker (`right-note-<inst>.chosen`; own round settings in `ninja-<inst>`). Note flies in on the staff, optional real-instrument reference first (the clock pauses while it plays), hold 500 ms within ±50 cents → ninja slices it; time out → note drifts away, round continues. End: three celebration levels in words, never a score; teacher presses Space for the next child. `SAMPLES` = recordings per instrument in `sounds/<inst>/` (concert names, measured cents off). `?test=1` + `testTone(writtenMidi, cents)`. A hidden Browser pane throttles rAF to ~3 fps; for timing tests, swap `requestAnimationFrame` for a MessageChannel pump.
- `songs.html` — whole songs, one child. `SONGS` = melodies as "step:beats" in 4/4, bars split by `|` (steps index the instrument's right-note note list, so trombone plays in B♭ with a 2-flat key signature, trumpet/horn in written C). A song is listed only if all its steps are ticked in the shared picker. Follow mode: hold 250 ms per note; a repeated pitch needs a new attack (short-window envelope dip), or just a re-hold with the "מספיק להחזיק" setting. In time: one-bar count-in, no sound while playing, notes judged by when the sound was made (`DETECT_LAG_MS`), a repeat needs its own attack. Listen-first plays the real recordings (loops the middle for long notes). Section = bar range. When the Saints was left out: no published melody was found to confirm the middle phrase.
- `clap-along.html` — rhythm game. Sections: rhythm model (`LEVELS`, `STARTS`, generator, quiz distractors) → SVG notation → Web Audio (`Sound`) → game state and wiring.
- `fonts/Bravura.woff2` — music symbol font (SIL OFL), needed offline for Note Names.
- `sw.js` — network-first with 3 s timeout, cache fallback.
- `manifest.json`, `icons/` — iPad home-screen install.
